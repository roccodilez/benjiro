# Project Goals

**Updated:** 2026-11-11

1. **Preserve continuity:** Maintain a clear record of Benjiro's characterization, events, relationships, and confirmed retcons.
2. **Support evidence-based investigation:** Keep verified evidence and timelines separate from theories in chapter mysteries.
3. **Organize creative assets:** Store approved artwork, references, documents, and source material with an index.
4. **Make cross-chat handoffs practical:** A new agent should be able to determine the current state and next action with minimal reconstruction.
5. **Learn from prior work:** Record decisions, corrections, and successful working practices.

The user remains the final authority for story scope and corrections.
